Corporate Overview

Nehemiah Solutions LLC is a registered Woman-Owned Small Business (WOSB) and Small Disadvantaged Business (SDB) based in Kent, Washington. We provide operational, administrative, program coordination, customer support, and project support services to government agencies, prime contractors, and commercial organizations. With over 21 years of enterprise experience across Fortune 500 healthcare, financial services, and telecommunications environments, we bring reliable execution, proactive accountability, and flexible capacity to every requirement.

Core Competencies

  • Administrative Operations: Workflow design, records management, calendar coordination, executive office management.
  • Program & Project Support: Milestone tracking, action-item management, documentation, closeout.
  • Stakeholder & Customer Support: Constituent outreach, inquiry triage, client communication management.
  • Travel & Logistics Coordination: Itineraries, lodging, transport, conference and event logistics.
  • Transaction Support: Document tracking, real estate coordination, scheduling and deadline control.
